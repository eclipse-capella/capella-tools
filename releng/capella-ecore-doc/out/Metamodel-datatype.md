modeling of structured data types

## ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/eclipse/capella/master/core/plugins/org.polarsys.capella.core.data.res.edit/icons/full/obj16/BooleanType.gif) BooleanType

[![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/eclipse-platform/eclipse.platform.images/master/org.eclipse.images/eclipse-png/org.eclipse.jdt.ui/icons/full/obj16/annotation_obj.png)](# "semantic")

A boolean is an instance of PrimitiveType. In the metamodel, Boolean defines an enumeration that denotes a logical
condition. Its enumeration literals are:
- true - The Boolean condition is satisfied.
- false - The Boolean condition is not satisfied
[source: UML superstructure v2.2]

A Dimension (SysML notion of Physical Quantity) is a kind of quantity that may be stated by means of defined units. For example, the dimension of length may be measured by units of meters, kilometers, or feet
